Our mission

Fathers leading their families in faith.

Dads for Christ exists to encourage fathers to lead their homes, love their families, strengthen their faith, and boldly share the Gospel in a world that desperately needs Christ.

Why we exist

Jesus at the center of the home.

We believe fathers have one of the most important callings on earth. Strong fathers build strong families, and strong families build strong communities. Our mission is to help dads put Jesus at the center of their homes and inspire other men to do the same.

Reach One

Inspired by Luke 15:4–7

“What man of you, having an hundred sheep, if he lose one of them, doth not leave the ninety and nine in the wilderness, and go after that which is lost, until he find it?”

Luke 15:4

The Reach One bracelet serves as a daily reminder that every soul matters. Jesus left the ninety-nine to seek the one lost sheep.

Wear the bracelet. Start conversations. Share the Gospel. Reach One.
