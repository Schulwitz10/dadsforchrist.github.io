Why Reach One?

Luke 15 tells us about the shepherd who left the ninety-nine to search for the one lost sheep. Reach One is more than a bracelet. It is a reminder to pray for one person, encourage one person, and share the hope of Jesus with one person.

Wear it as a reminder. Give one to someone else. Start a conversation. Reach One.
