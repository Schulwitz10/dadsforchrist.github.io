Questions

Are gifts tax-deductible?

Gifts are currently not tax-deductible until our 501(c)(3) approval is complete.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Yes. Every monthly plan includes a manage-subscription link in your receipt, so you can change or cancel anytime.

Is online giving secure?

Yes. All payments are processed by a PCI-compliant provider over encrypted connections. We never store your card info on our site.

Why do some buttons say placeholder?

The previous donate page pointed at unfinished Stripe links (YOUR_ONETIME_LINK and YOUR_MONTHLY_LINK). Those were not live checkouts. This page keeps giving visible, and it will not pretend a button can take a payment until a real link is saved in donations.json.
